2. Legal basis

We process your email address on the basis of your consent, given when you tick the consent box on the pre-registration form. Analytics cookies are set only after you press Accept in the cookie banner.

3. Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to understand how the page is used. Nothing is loaded and no cookie is set until you press Accept in the banner. If you press Decline, the analytics script is never downloaded.

Because this service is intended for families with children, we have disabled advertising features entirely: Google signals and ad personalisation are turned off, and IP addresses are anonymised. Collected data is never used for advertising and is never sold.

You can change your mind at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser, which makes the banner appear again. You may also install Google's opt-out browser add-on. Declining has no effect on pre-registration.
