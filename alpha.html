Peera
iOS alpha · TestFlight

Write. Draw. Make a storybook.

Peera is a children's writing app where Writy, a small squirrel character, calls first. A child can talk about their day in Korean or English, then write a diary or story directly on an iPad using a stylus or their finger.

Children can also turn up to 8 of their own stories into a storybook by drawing a cover, adding a title, and publishing it to their bookshelf.

What you need

Recommended:

  • An iPad (iPadOS 15.0 or later)
  • Apple Pencil or another stylus

This alpha is built around iPad + Apple Pencil, and that's the experience we're most curious to hear about. Peera also runs on iPhone, and there's a typing mode if you'd rather not handwrite — so it's not a hard requirement, just not the main thing this round is testing.

A few things to know before you join

By installing/joining the alpha, you agree that:

  • This is an alpha version, so bugs and errors may occur.
  • We may occasionally contact you by email to ask about your experience. We will not contact you frequently or use this as a promotional mailing list.
  • Please join only if you're comfortable testing an early version and encountering things that may not work perfectly yet.

How long can I use it?

The alpha is free to use for at least 90 days. The current TestFlight build is set to expire 90 days after its upload on August 25, 2026. This is a TestFlight platform limitation, not a period we're defining as a commercial free trial.

If we need to end the alpha earlier than that, we will notify participants at least 7 days in advance.

Korean and English

Peera supports Korean and English conversations and writing. If you're learning Korean, you're welcome to try it as well.

One note: the English UI is still being worked on. Some buttons, menus, settings, or parent-facing screens may still appear in Korean while we're improving the interface.
